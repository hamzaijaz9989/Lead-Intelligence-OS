# Decision Log

## D-001 — Demo bookings are the north-star outcome
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Decision:** Optimise the product for maximising qualified ExactFlow demos booked, not raw lead count.  
**Reason:** The system exists to produce sales conversations and demos.  
**Implication:** Track source-to-demo conversion and prioritise evidence-backed ICP quality.

## D-002 — Global scope
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Decision:** No fixed country restriction; no fixed industry restriction where a business credibly fits ExactFlow's physical-product operational ICP.  
**Reason:** The best opportunities may exist outside initial target markets.  
**Implication:** Prioritise by fit, evidence, contactability, compliance and observed conversion.

## D-003 — Multi-source prospecting
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Decision:** Consider search, company websites, professional networks, marketplaces, directories, communities, social channels, job postings and freelance marketplaces such as Fiverr/Upwork where permitted.  
**Reason:** Operational hiring and service requests can reveal buying signals.  
**Implication:** Check source terms and do not treat marketplace signals as permission to solicit.

## D-004 — Manual outbound in MVP
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Decision:** System prepares channel-specific outreach but does not automatically call, email, text or message.  
**Reason:** Human control, compliance and quality.  
**Implication:** Approval and activity logging are required.

## D-005 — Nine logical agents, shared platform
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Decision:** Model nine coordinated agent roles, not nine standalone services/apps.  
**Reason:** Reduce complexity and cost while retaining clear responsibilities.  
**Implication:** Shared evidence store, orchestrator and common policy controls.

## D-006 — Evidence before claims
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Decision:** Store source provenance and distinguish fact, hypothesis, unknown and contradiction. Never fabricate contact details or pain points.  
**Reason:** Sales credibility and data integrity.  
**Implication:** Qualified dossiers need evidence links and confidence.

## D-007 — Maintain a living project record
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Decision:** Update Master Project Record, relevant specs, milestone status and changelog after every meaningful change.  
**Reason:** Preserve project continuity across sessions.  
**Implication:** Documentation is part of definition of done.

## D-008 — Build together in the user's GitHub
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Decision:** ChatGPT and user collaborate directly in the connected GitHub repository; do not make Codex or Opus the primary builder.  
**Reason:** Explicit user preference.  
**Implication:** Use connected GitHub operations; be transparent about local testing limits.

## D-009 — No implementation before milestone agreement
**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Decision:** Planning/documentation is allowed now; application implementation begins only after Milestone 1 scope and acceptance criteria are agreed.  
**Reason:** User requested agreement before coding.  
**Implication:** Present M1 for approval before writing app code.
