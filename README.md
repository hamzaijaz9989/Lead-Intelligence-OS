# Lead Intelligence OS

**A global, evidence-driven B2B prospect intelligence and demo-generation workspace for ExactFlow.**

The primary business outcome is to maximise qualified ExactFlow demos booked. The system discovers companies worldwide, researches their operational complexity, identifies relevant decision-makers and permissible business contact routes, prepares personalised sales strategies, and helps the human sales operator manage outreach and learn from outcomes.

## Non-negotiable principles

- Demo bookings are the north-star outcome; lead volume alone is not success.
- Global prospecting: no fixed country restriction.
- Prioritise evidence-backed ICP fit and operational complexity.
- Never invent companies, people, contact details, technology usage or business problems.
- Distinguish verified facts from hypotheses and record source evidence.
- Public contact information does not automatically grant marketing permission.
- Human approval is required for external outreach and consequential actions.
- Keep the initial build lean and cost-conscious; disclose where free data sources cannot provide reliable coverage.
- Update the Master Project Record, relevant documentation and changelog after every meaningful project change.
